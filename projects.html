Projects

Projects

Structure-based drug discovery work centered on C. albicans Als3, plus the toolkit behind it.

Antifungal & Antibiofilm Docking against Als3

Large-scale structure-based screening of a curated antifungal/antibiofilm library against C. albicans Als3 (PDB 4LEB). Scaffold classification (ClassyFire, Murcko decomposition via RDKit) and residue-interaction analysis across 228 diagrams revealed a weak score–activity correlation, pointing to mechanistic heterogeneity across the library.

Molecular Docking Als3 DFT / HOMO–LUMO

Dual-Engine Consensus Docking

Built a PharmaCoNet + AutoDock Vina consensus framework, with Python-based RMSD validation, to satisfy a reviewer's request for stronger confidence in Als3 binding pose predictions on a manuscript now under revision.

Consensus Docking RMSD Validation CADD

Natural Carotenoid Screening against Als3

First published study to screen natural carotenoids against Als3, identifying mangicrocin as a top hit and establishing a starting point for the later antifungal library and consensus-docking work.
