MSA University — where the work started 2019 – 2023

BSc in Biotechnological Sciences at October University for Modern Sciences and Arts, 6th of October City. Graduated with First Class Honours. This is where I first met molecular modelling, and where the thread that became my Als3 work began.

University of Greenwich — the awarding body BSc (Hons) conferred 2023

My programme in Egypt was validated by the University of Greenwich, so the same four years of study were examined to Greenwich standards, moderated by Greenwich academics, and conferred as a British BSc (Hons) alongside the Egyptian degree.

I sat every exam in Cairo. The degree crossed the Channel; I never did — which is exactly why this point is drawn hollow and linked by a dashed line rather than a flight path.
