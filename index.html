About
"[add a short research philosophy or motivating quote here]"

I am a Master's student in computational medicinal chemistry, working at the intersection of medicinal chemistry and structure-based drug design. My research centers on Candida albicans Als3 (PDB: 4LEB), a cell-surface adhesin protein involved in biofilm formation, as a target for new antifungal and antibiofilm agents. Across two manuscripts I've combined large-scale molecular docking, DFT/HOMO–LUMO analysis, scaffold classification, and a dual-engine consensus docking framework (PharmaCoNet + AutoDock Vina) to screen, validate, and rationalize candidate compounds against this target.

I am currently based in [your city/university], South Korea — [add your program, university, and lab/advisor here]. I also serve as a peer reviewer, most recently for an A. baumannii docking manuscript, and I'm preparing to apply for a DPhil in bioinformatics or synthetic biology at the University of Oxford.

My work has been published as first author in Molecular Diversity and as second author in the European Journal of Medicinal Chemistry.

Research

Research highlights

Antifungal & Antibiofilm Docking against Als3

Large-scale structure-based screening of a curated antifungal/antibiofilm library against C. albicans Als3 (PDB 4LEB). Scaffold classification (ClassyFire, Murcko decomposition via RDKit) and residue-interaction analysis across 228 diagrams revealed a weak score–activity correlation, pointing to mechanistic heterogeneity across the library.

Molecular Docking Als3 DFT / HOMO–LUMO

Dual-Engine Consensus Docking

Built a PharmaCoNet + AutoDock Vina consensus framework, with Python-based RMSD validation, to satisfy a reviewer's request for stronger confidence in Als3 binding pose predictions on a manuscript now under revision.

Consensus Docking RMSD Validation CADD

Natural Carotenoid Screening against Als3

First published study to screen natural carotenoids against Als3, identifying mangicrocin as a top hit and establishing a starting point for the later antifungal library and consensus-docking work.
