About
"[add a short research philosophy or motivating quote here]"

I am a Master's student in computational medicinal chemistry, working at the intersection of medicinal chemistry and structure-based drug design. My research centers on Candida albicans Als3 (PDB: 4LEB), a cell-surface adhesin protein involved in biofilm formation, as a target for new antifungal and antibiofilm agents. Across two manuscripts I've combined large-scale molecular docking, DFT/HOMO–LUMO analysis, scaffold classification, and a dual-engine consensus docking framework (PharmaCoNet + AutoDock Vina) to screen, validate, and rationalize candidate compounds against this target.

I also serve as a peer reviewer, most recently for an A. baumannii docking manuscript, and I'm preparing to apply for a DPhil in bioinformatics or synthetic biology at the University of Oxford.
