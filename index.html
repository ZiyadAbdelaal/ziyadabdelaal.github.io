About
"[add a short research philosophy or motivating quote here]"

I am a Master's student in computational medicinal chemistry, working at the intersection of medicinal chemistry and structure-based drug design. My research centers on Candida albicans Als3, a cell-surface adhesin protein involved in biofilm formation, as a target for new antifungal and antibiofilm agents. I use molecular docking, dual-engine consensus docking, and related computational methods to screen and validate candidate compound libraries against this target.
